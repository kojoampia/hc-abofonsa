What we're building

One record. Every point of care.

Care breaks down in the gaps between providers. Abofonsa closes them with a shared, consent-driven record and the tooling to act on it.

Unified patient record

Visits, labs, prescriptions and referrals in one longitudinal timeline — readable at any facility in the network.

Referrals that arrive

Send a patient onward with their full history attached, and track whether they were actually seen.

Consent by default

Patients decide who sees what. Every access is logged, scoped and revocable — no silent sharing.

Signals for the system

Aggregate, de‑identified insight so districts and programmes can see demand and outcomes as they move.

Works offline‑first

Built for real connectivity. Record now, sync when the network returns — nothing is lost in between.

Open standards

FHIR‑aligned APIs so existing HMIS, lab and pharmacy systems can plug in rather than be replaced.
